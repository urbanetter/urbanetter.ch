Ursi Collective is a playground for collaborations with friends. We mix generative art, storytelling, and interaction design to build small experiences that invite curiosity.

One of the ongoing experiments is a chess visualiser written in p5.js that replays famous games with bespoke illustrations. It is equal parts code study and mood piece.
