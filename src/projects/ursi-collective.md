Ursi Collective is a playground for collaborations with [Probiere bringts](https://www.instagram.com/probiere_bringts/). We mix generative art, storytelling, and interaction design to build small experiences that invite curiosity.

One of the ongoing experiments is abstract chess visualiser written in p5.js that replays one games with bespoke illustrations. It is equal parts code study and mood piece.

Click on the board to advance the game.
